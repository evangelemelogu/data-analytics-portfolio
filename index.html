About Me

I bring together two strengths: a solid background in banking and operations management, and the analytical skills to turn business data into meaningful insights. Having worked closely with customers, teams and operational processes, I understand that behind every performance report is a business challenge that needs attention. Whether it's delayed service delivery, inefficient processes, growing backlogs or missed performance targets, my interest goes beyond identifying the numbers to understanding what they mean for the business. As a Data Analyst Consultant, I use SQL, Power BI, Tableau and Excel to investigate these challenges, uncover patterns and translate complex information into clear, actionable recommendations. My portfolio reflects this approach through projects in customer support, healthcare operations, telecommunications and business performance. What drives my work is simple: helping organisations move from knowing what is happening to understanding what needs to happen next.

FEATURED PROJECTS

NorthBridge Customer Support Analytics

Customer support performance is not just about how many tickets a team handles. It is also about how quickly customers receive help, how effectively unresolved requests are managed, and whether service performance is being reported accurately.

In this project, I used SQL Server and Power BI to analyse 3,500 customer support tickets, taking the data through validation, cleaning, analysis and interactive dashboard development.

My objective was to understand ticket demand, service delays, backlog exposure, SLA performance, client activity and agent workload, and translate those findings into information that could support better operational decisions.

KEY ANALYTICAL FINDINGS

01 | SLA Reporting Gaps

119 overdue backlog tickets were not flagged as SLA breaches, highlighting a potential weakness in how service performance was being monitored and reported.

02 | Backlog and Service Delays

The analysis examined outstanding tickets alongside response times and SLA indicators, helping identify areas requiring closer operational attention.

03 | Agent Workload and Performance

Agent-level reporting brought ticket workload and service performance into view, providing a basis for reviewing workload distribution and support-team effectiveness.

04 | Client Activity and Ticket Demand

Ticket patterns and client activity were analysed to give management greater visibility into service demand and where further investigation may be needed.

BUSINESS VALUE

Across four dashboard sections, I brought together operational KPIs and performance indicators to help stakeholders identify reporting inconsistencies, monitor service delivery, prioritise outstanding requests and make more informed decisions about customer support operations.

HORIZON HEALTHCARE ANALYSIS

Healthcare Operations | Capacity | Staffing | Patient Flow

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Effective healthcare delivery depends on more than the number of patients admitted. Hospital managers must also understand whether sufficient beds, staff and operational resources are available to meet patient demand.

In this project, I used SQL Server and Tableau to analyse Horizon Healthcare's operational data, examining how patient admissions, bed capacity and staffing levels interact across hospitals and wards.

After validating, cleaning and structuring the data in SQL Server, I developed five interactive Tableau dashboards to provide visibility into hospital performance, staffing shortages, capacity pressures and patient flow.

KEY ANALYTICAL FINDINGS

01

Nursing Workforce Shortfall

94,931

Nurses recorded the largest staffing shortfall, highlighting a significant workforce planning concern.

02

Consultant Staffing Attainment

71.82%

Consultants recorded the lowest staffing attainment rate, indicating a substantial gap between required and available staffing.

03

Bed Capacity and Occupancy

Ward-level analysis examined bed occupancy and available capacity to identify areas where patient demand may place greater pressure on hospital resources.

04

Operational Risk and Priority Wards

Staffing gaps and occupancy indicators were brought together to highlight wards requiring closer monitoring and management attention.

BUSINESS VALUE

The analysis provides hospital decision-makers with a clearer understanding of where staffing resources are falling short, where bed capacity is under pressure and which operational areas require priority attention. By bringing these indicators together across five dashboards, the project supports more informed workforce planning, capacity management and operational decision-making.

STREAMWAVE ENTERTAINMENT

Viewer Engagement | Subscriber Retention | Content Performance

PROJECT OVERVIEW

High viewing numbers do not always mean that content is performing well. For streaming platforms, understanding what audiences watch is only part of the picture. It is equally important to understand whether viewers complete content, return for more and remain engaged.

In this project, I used Excel to analyse 60,000 viewing records across 500 content titles, nine genres and 999 users. My objective was to understand viewing behaviour, audience engagement, subscriber activity and content efficiency.

I developed two interactive Excel dashboards to examine genre performance, content completion, repeat viewing, subscriber retention and viewing efficiency. The analysis revealed important differences between content popularity and the quality of audience engagement.

KEY ANALYTICAL FINDINGS

01

Audience Reach and Engagement

Drama & Comedy

These genres generated strong overall viewing activity and repeat engagement, demonstrating their importance in attracting and retaining audience attention.

02

Content Completion Behaviour

Biography

Although smaller in audience size, Biography demonstrated stronger completion behaviour among the viewers it attracted, highlighting the value of looking beyond total views.

03

Content Efficiency

Action

Action stood out when performance was assessed at individual title level, showing why viewing hours alone may not provide a complete basis for content investment decisions.

04

Subscriber Retention and Investment

The analysis brought together subscriber activity, viewing hours per title and subscription patterns to help identify opportunities for improving content investment and audience retention.

BUSINESS VALUE

The findings demonstrate why streaming platforms should evaluate content using a combination of audience reach, completion behaviour, repeat engagement and content efficiency. This provides a stronger foundation for deciding which genres deserve further investment, where content portfolios may need improvement and how to support long-term subscriber engagement.

BRITCONNECT TELECOMMUNICATIONS

Network Reliability | Operational Performance | Customer Experience

PROJECT OVERVIEW

In telecommunications, an outage is more than a technical disruption. It can affect network reliability, customer experience and the provider's ability to meet service commitments.

Using SQL Server and Power BI, I analysed network outages, equipment failures, maintenance activities and customer service data. I developed three interactive dashboards to examine network performance, operational reliability and customer impact.

NETWORK PERFORMANCE AT A GLANCE

5K Network Outages
183K Downtime Minutes
1M+ Customers Affected

KEY ANALYTICAL FINDINGS

01 / NETWORK RELIABILITY

London recorded the highest total downtime

London accounted for approximately 49,652 downtime minutes, while Scotland recorded the highest average downtime per outage. This distinction helps identify where disruption is greatest overall and where individual incidents last longer.

02 / EQUIPMENT PERFORMANCE

Hardware failures were a major source of downtime

Hardware-related failures contributed approximately 49,590 downtime minutes. This highlights an opportunity to investigate equipment reliability and prioritise preventive maintenance.

03 / SERVICE LEVEL PERFORMANCE

19.58% of outages breached SLA commitments

The analysis identified 979 SLA breaches. This represents nearly one in five recorded outages and highlights the importance of improving incident detection, response and service restoration.

04 / CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Network reliability and customer outcomes need a connected view

Customer complaints, complaint resolution and SLA performance were analysed alongside network incidents to help identify where service disruptions may require greater customer-facing attention.

BUSINESS VALUE

The project brings technical network performance and customer experience into one decision-making view. It supports better prioritisation of high-impact regions, investigation of recurring equipment failures, more targeted maintenance planning and closer monitoring of service commitments.
